What the model estimates

The semi-analytical workflow uses satellite remote-sensing reflectance to estimate shallow-water depth and bottom composition. The displayed products are model outputs from Sentinel-2 scenes.

Processing sequence

1. Prepare aligned visible-band remote-sensing reflectance (Rrs).
2. Estimate water-column optical properties using the HOPE/PGX model.
3. Generate candidate depth and bottom-mixture parameters.
4. Select promising initial candidates and optimize the spectral fit.
5. Export estimated depth, sand and seagrass fractions, and habitat class.

The fit to satellite spectra is a model diagnostic. It does not establish accuracy against measured depth or habitat observations.

Explore and inspect

Open the satellite results map or open the image viewer. The Python implementation is in run_all.py and model.py.
