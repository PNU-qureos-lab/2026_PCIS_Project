What was done

Easy concept: surface glint tends to brighten both a visible band and the 866 nm NIR band. Hedley learns their relationship in selected open water and subtracts the NIR-linked component from each visible band.
ρ′λ = ρλ − bλ × (ρ866 − min(ρ866))
  • Published part: the equation, the minimum training-water NIR reference, and ordinary least-squares visible–NIR slopes.
  • Project-specific selection: training water is automatically chosen from UDM2-clear water at least 300 m from land-like pixels, with a simple deep-water spectral screen.
  • Not used: no percentile reference, no positive-only truncation, no correction-strength scaling, and no clipping of negative reflectance.
Input limitation: these are Planet 6S Surface Reflectance products, not dedicated aquatic atmospheric-correction products. Corrected SR is the primary output. Rrs = SR/π and subsurface rrs are supplied only as explicitly labelled approximations.

Complete workflow

1 · InputRead eight-band Planet SR and UDM2.
2 · MaskExclude land, cloud, haze, shadow and invalid pixels.
3 · TrainSelect documented offshore open-water pixels.
4 · FitFit one ordinary least-squares slope per 444–707 nm band against 866 nm.
5 · CorrectApply the standard Hedley equation without scaling.
6 · EvaluateReport negatives, residual NIR relation and spatial preservation.
