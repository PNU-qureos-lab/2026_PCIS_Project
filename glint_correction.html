Sentinel-2 glint correction

The included processing code aligns bands to a common grid, uses water and quality masks, estimates glint from shortwave infrared information, and converts corrected reflectance for downstream analysis.

  1. Prepare the 20 m grid.
  2. Apply the glint correction.
  3. Convert corrected reflectance.
  4. Export four-band and shallow-area products.
